A LITTLE ABOUT ME

K.

I’m Kat Bassett, a designer with a practical streak and a love for turning ideas into clear, thoughtful visuals.

My experience spans graphic and web design, from custom labels and print materials to signage, social media graphics and accessible web pages. I enjoy working to a brief, taking feedback on board and refining the details until the design feels right.

Alongside design, I bring more than ten years of experience across systems analysis, administration and software development. That technical background helps me balance creativity with structure, problem-solving and an eye for how things work in the real world.

I’ve worked with Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop, created designs to client specifications, and designed and updated web pages to established visual styles and accessibility standards. I hold a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science from Lancaster University.
